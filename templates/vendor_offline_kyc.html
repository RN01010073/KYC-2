Document requirements
  • KYC form must be the official vendor KYC form — fully completed, signed, and company-stamped
  • All scans/photos must be clear, well-lit, and show all four corners of the document
  • Aadhaar uploads must show both front and back sides — the bottom address side is required
  • Cancelled cheque must have the account number and IFSC pre-printed (not handwritten)
  • Documents issued more than 3 months ago for address proof may be rejected
  • Non-English documents must include a notarised English translation
