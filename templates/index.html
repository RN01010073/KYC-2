How it works
Verify in 4 steps
1

User submits ID document

Capture via mobile camera or file upload. Supports JPEG, PNG, and PDF formats.

2

AI extracts and validates data

OCR reads document fields, MRZ checks integrity, and models flag tampering or forgery.

3

Liveness and face match

A short selfie video confirms the person is live and matches the document photo.

4

Result delivered via webhook

Pass/fail decision with confidence scores, risk flags, and extracted data — in under 3 seconds.
